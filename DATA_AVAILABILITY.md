# Data availability and provenance

The publicly shareable materials are the theoretical codebook, a **reconstructed reproducibility prompt and implementation**, sample synthetic input, and audit code. Original annotated political/media texts, identifiable reviewer decisions, and secrets are deliberately excluded.

For archival verification of the originally published historical experiment, the responsible author should separately deposit: the exact historical prompt, API model snapshot, month/year, request/response logs, checksum of source inputs, and reviewer decisions under the applicable permissions. Those records were not present in the available source files when this package was assembled.

The four historical JSON files supplied for private audit comprised 256 paragraph records and 1,324 assigned label occurrences, including 447 `Unspecified` values and 12 out-of-taxonomy occurrences. These totals are not confidence scores or error rates, and **should not be interpreted as a verified gold standard**.
